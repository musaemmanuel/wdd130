Adamawa, Nigeria

Adamawa State, officially nicknamed the "Land of Beauty," is a prominent state located in the North-East geopolitical zone of Nigeria. Known for its breathtaking mountainous landscapes, rich cultural heritage, and remarkable ethnic diversity, it is one of the largest states in the country by landmass. Situated in northeastern Nigeria, it shares internal borders with Borno State to the northwest, Gombe State to the west, and Taraba State,Jalingo to the southwest.

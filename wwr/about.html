White Water Rafting





At Exciting Expeditions, we are passionate about providing thrill-seekers with an unforgettable whitewater rafting experience. Our expert guides ensure your safety while you navigate stunning landscapes and challenging rapids. Oars up!

History

Exciting Expeditions was founded in 1998 by a small group of river guides who wanted to share their love of the outdoors. What started as a single raft and a handful of loyal customers has grown over the years.

Today we are one of the region's most trusted whitewater adventure companies, guiding thousands of paddlers safely down the rapids every season.

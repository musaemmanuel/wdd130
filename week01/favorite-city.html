My Favorite City

Abuja is the capital city of Nigeria and the Federal Capital Territory (FCT). It is located in central Nigeria and is known for its relatively planned layout, broad roads, government institutions, modern buildings, and surrounding hills and greenery. What makes Abuja especially interesting is the combination of urban development and natural scenery. One of its most recognizable landmarks is Aso Rock, which overlooks much of the city. The city also has attractions such as the National Mosque, Millennium Park, Jabi Lake, and various recreational and cultural centres. Millennium Park is one of the city's notable green spaces, while Jabi Lake Park Abuja provides a recreational setting around Jabi Lake.
